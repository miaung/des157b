Your privacy is protected ... kind of.

The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) protects your health records.

The Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) safeguards your sensitive financial data.

The California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) gives residents some say over their personal data.

But currently, in the U.S., there's no single, comprehensive national privacy law.

Our current laws don't stop companies from collecting, selling, or sharing your data.

That means your clicks. Your purchases. Your movements. Your data.

Companies profit from your digital footprint.

You just don't get a cut.

So what if you could?

What if your privacy could be monetized?
